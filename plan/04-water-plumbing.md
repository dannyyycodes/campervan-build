# 04 — Water & plumbing (minimal, removable)

**Spec (locked):** NO fixed tanks. Food-grade fresh **can**, small sink + pump, grey **can**. Everything lifts out.

## Fresh water can — **2 × 20–25L** (not one 40L: weight + redundancy)
- **Stainless (max non-toxic):** Swiss Link / Guzzle 20L 304 stainless, ~£70–90. Inert, no taste, lasts forever, heavier.
- **BPA-free HDPE (pragmatic):** opaque food-grade 25L jerry, ~£15. Light, cheap. **Choose opaque** (blocks light → slows algae) + **food-grade** (not fuel-grade).
- Two cans = one in use, one spare; rotate + sterilise. **Decision: stainless vs HDPE (sign-off).**

## Pump — **Whale Babyfoot foot pump (~£35)** primary
Best for the low-EMF / low-power brief: **zero electricity, zero standby field, near-silent, can't leave a tap running.** ~8 L/min, floor-mounted, 13mm barb.
- **Optional 12V upgrade:** Seaflo diaphragm pump + 0.75L accumulator (pressure-on, hands-free) — but adds wiring, noise, standby draw, EMF, and a slipped hose can silently empty a can. A want, not a need.

## Sink + tap + grey
- Small **stainless** bowl sink (~250–300mm) — hygienic, no mould seams.
- Cold-only tap (foot pump = straight on/off; 12V = lever/microswitch).
- Grey: a **second can, different colour/labelled** (20–25L, ~£12), short 19–25mm waste hose. **Empty daily** (goes stale fast).

## Fittings & hygiene
- 12–13mm **food-grade hose + barb fittings + stainless jubilee clips**, or PEX push-fit. Fewest joints, all **visible** under the counter (spot drips).
- Sterilise fresh can monthly (Milton / Puriclean). Opaque/stainless slows algae.

## Winterising — the can-system's hidden win
**Nothing plumbed to freeze-burst.** Just drain the pump + hose, bring the fresh can indoors on a hard frost, store grey empty. No antifreeze, no blow-out kit, no split tank.

## Schematic
`[Fresh can] → Babyfoot foot pump → cold tap @ small SS sink → gravity drain → [Grey can]`
(Optional parallel: fresh can → 12V Seaflo → accumulator → lever tap, fused.)

## In the model
Fresh + grey cans in the rear garage under the bed (`Water_Cans`); sink in the kitchen unit.
