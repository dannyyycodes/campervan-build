# 06 — Facilities & toilet (facility-hop)

**Spec (locked):** NO fixed on-board toilet, NO black tank. Facility-hop + emergency backup. Showers = luxury gyms / Muay Thai gyms (primary) + portable shower in the awning (secondary).

## Emergency in-van backup (layered, all bin-disposable)
1. **Collapsible bucket toilet** (folds ~2", ~2.4kg) lined with a **heavy-duty bin bag** → seal + bin in general waste. Primary backup. (UK chemical-disposal points are scarce/seasonal — that's *why* we avoid a wet Porta-Potti.)
2. **WAG / gel bags** (gelling + deodorising powder, solidifies liquid) — the always-there glovebox fallback.
3. **Pee bottle / FUD** — discreet nights.

## Finding toilets + showers (apps)
- **Flush** — 200,000+ toilets, filters, **works offline**. Primary toilet finder.
- **Park4Night** — overnight spots + nearby toilets/showers/water/services.
- **Search for Sites** — UK-stronger P4N alternative; run both.
- Showers: **PureGym / The Gym Group** rolling memberships; leisure-centre **£3–6 day passes** include showers; P4N tags public showers (aires, marinas).

## Daily routine
- **Morning:** #2 + shower at the gym, or a supermarket/leisure-centre/café toilet on the first stop; empty pee bottle.
- **Day:** toilet-hop — supermarkets, petrol stations, leisure centres, cafés (buy a coffee), McDonald's, libraries.
- **Evening:** last toilet stop before parking; bucket-toilet/WAG bag + pee bottle as night fallback.
- **Housekeeping:** bin used bags, **empty grey-water can** at a foul drain, top up fresh can at any tap.

## Portable shower (awning)
- **NEMO Helio (11L, foot-pump pressurised, ~5–7min spray, ~£90)** — no power/EMF, stands on the awning floor, trigger nozzle saves water. (Helio LX 22L for longer washes.)
- **Warm water with no fixed heater:** boil a kettle/pan on the hob, mix to temp in the Helio, pressurise → warm awning shower.
- **Privacy:** the drive-away awning IS the cubicle (or NEMO Heliopolis pop-up tent with French-drain floor).
- **Grey water:** shower over a collapsible tray → grey can, OR on a draining mat onto permeable ground away from watercourses, **biodegradable soap only**.
