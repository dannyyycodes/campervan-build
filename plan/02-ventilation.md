# 02 — Ventilation & condensation

The single highest-leverage anti-mould system. Breathing (~0.5–1L water/night), cooking (gas adds **~450mL water/hour**), and the diesel heater all load the air — only real **air exchange** removes it. Core of the non-toxic/mould-free brief.

## Roof fan (the centrepiece)
- **MaxxFan Deluxe** — 10-speed, **intake AND exhaust**, built-in rain cover (run it in rain), thermostat, remote. Consensus pick. ~£200–300.
- **Cut-out 14″×14″ (≈360×360mm)** — cut during the metal phase, before insulation/cladding.
- **Place over the KITCHEN/galley, NOT the bed** — that's where the moisture + the only combustion fumes are. For sleeping cross-flow: fan on low exhaust + **cracked window at the bed end** pulls air across the van.

## Passive make-up-air vent (mandatory)
A powered fan with no inlet just depressurises. Fit a **low-level passive vent** (floor/sill mushroom or low wall louvre), ideally at the **opposite end to the fan** → permanent low-in / high-out path that runs even shut. Also the combustion make-up air.

## Operating rules
- **Sleeping:** fan low exhaust + cracked bed-end window (clears the nightly ~0.5–1L).
- **Cooking:** fan **high exhaust** + window cracked + **lids on pans** (biggest vapour reducer). Gas dumps ~450mL/hr + CO — extract hard.
- **Working all day:** passive vent open + fan low trickle. Wool buffers humidity, airflow removes it.
- No carpet (holds damp). Wipe morning condensation off cold spots (window frames).

## CO / combustion safety (non-negotiable)
- **Diesel heater:** sealed combustion — intake + exhaust both routed **outside**, exhaust at the floorpan edge pointing down/away (never under centre where CO pools near the intake). Correctly installed = zero cabin CO.
- **Gas hob:** open flame burns O₂ + dumps water + CO into the cabin — the bigger source. Always fan + window when lit.
- **CO alarm (EN 50291-2, RV/vibration-rated) mounted HIGH at sleeping head-height** + ideally a 2nd at the galley.
- **LPG alarm (EN 50194) mounted LOW** if running gas (propane sinks).
