# 09 — Materials (non-toxic spec)

The whole build is non-toxic + breathable + low-EMF. This is the sacred materials list — nothing off-gassing, nothing that traps moisture.

## Locked
- **Insulation:** sheep wool (Thermafleece / Havelock). NO spray foam, NO PIR-only sealed builds, NO Dynamat.
- **Ply:** formaldehyde-free / low-formaldehyde **birch ply** (carcasses, lining).
- **Finishes:** zero-VOC paint, natural-oil / hardwax-oil wood finishes.
- **Floor:** cork or natural finish — **no carpet** (mould/allergen).
- **Bed:** organic wool or natural-latex mattress, wool duvet, organic cotton/linen sheets, wool pillows. No memory foam, no synthetic next to skin.
- **Adhesives/sealants:** low-VOC, solvent-free where possible.
- **EMF:** master kill-switch by bed; Ethernet over Wi-Fi; single-point chassis earth; twisted-pair DC; bed away from electrical components. Earthing/grounding sheet on the bed (to true earth via hookup / earth rod when parked).

## Why
Mould-resistance + air quality + sleep quality. Breathable wool + roof fan + no carpet = no trapped moisture. See [01-insulation](01-insulation.md), [02-ventilation](02-ventilation.md).
